THE REAL THING

How BrickSpeed works

The online racer is the warm-up. The real races happen on your floor with brick cars, a lap station and your phone.

1

The car

A brick chassis with a tiny ESP32 brain, a motor and a steering servo.

  • Gets updates over the air
  • Blinks its LED with the start lights
2

The station

Sits at the finish line and runs the race.

  • Start lights, laps and positions
  • Feels which car passes it
  • Runs every ability
3

The pad + your phone

Your phone is the controller and the game.

  • Lever, tilt or touch-wheel driving
  • Boxes, upgrades, the Pass
  • Wi-Fi or Bluetooth
SEASON 1

The BrickSpeed Pass

Earn XP from races and boxes. Every 100 XP is a new tier with a reward.

20 tiers. 2 tracks.

The free track gives coins, power points and boxes. The Pass track adds Legendary boxes, Level Up tickets, gems and titles like DRIFT KING and CHAMPION.

Coins & gems

Win coins in races. Change coins into gems (never back). Gems unlock the Pass for 120.
