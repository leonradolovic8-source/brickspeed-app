BRICKSPEED Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what the BrickSpeed app (Android) and the BrickSpeed online game collect and why. Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell anything about you.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your own BrickSpeed pad and drive your car. It does not use Bluetooth to find your location, and it does not look at other devices except BrickSpeed pads.

What stays on your phone

Your pad progress (coins, drivers, skins, levels), your settings and the nickname you choose are saved on your phone and on your BrickSpeed pad. They are not sent to us.

Online races

  • Nickname and race data: when you race online, your nickname, driver, skin and live race positions are sent to the other players in the same race.
  • Connecting players: to connect players, the game uses the PeerJS service (peerjs.com), which sees a random connection id and your internet address while you are online. It is not used for anything else.
  • Top 10 ranking: if you post a time, your nickname, time, track and driver are saved on brickspeed.fun and shown in the public ranking. To stop spam, your internet address is turned into a one-way code and forgotten after about one minute.

Pick a nickname that is not your real name. There is no chat.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No accounts, no email or phone number needed to play.
  • No location.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Children

BrickSpeed is a toy game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only thing a player types is a nickname. Parents can ask us to remove a nickname from the ranking at any time.

Delete your data

Clearing the app's data (or uninstalling it) removes everything saved on the phone. To remove a nickname from the Top 10, email us.
